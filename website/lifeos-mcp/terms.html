1. Description of Application

LifeOS MCP is an agentic AI bridge operating over the open Model Context Protocol (MCP). It translates direct user prompts into structured actions against authorized developer APIs, specifically Google Workspace services including Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Tasks.
